Phone mocks (work list): portrait 9:19.5, ≥750px wide.  Project cards: 16:10, ≥1200px wide.
Testimonials + pull quotes: edit SITE.quotes at the top of the script in index.html. Set SITE.hideEmpty=true to hide anything still unfilled.
